# Early Artifact Convoy Candidate

Status: recorded candidate; do not implement before the controlled two-chapter emotion audit.

## Core Event

Early in the campaign, the player encounters a badly wounded weapon convoy. The player may ambush it and take an obviously powerful artifact weapon. Taking the weapon immediately creates a five-star pursuit order.

## Intended Experience

The event should create an immediate power fantasy before asking the player to understand the game's deeper systems. The artifact is not a free tutorial grant: the player actively chooses to steal it, verifies its strength in combat, and accepts a visible future threat.

The desired loop is:

```text
discover convoy
-> understand the tempting reward and visible risk
-> choose whether to ambush
-> obtain and test the artifact
-> see pursuit pressure approach
-> form the goal of becoming strong enough to keep the artifact
-> engage with equipment, roster, and progression for a self-authored reason
```

## Why It May Work

- The artifact supplies early spectacle before precise lock-key teaching.
- Player choice gives the reward ownership and causal clarity.
- The pursuit order converts power into a durable goal instead of a one-time gift.
- Later equipment and character decisions become preparation for a threat the player knowingly created.

## Design Constraints

- The first pursuit must help demonstrate the artifact's strength, not immediately punish the choice.
- Pursuit pressure must be visible and forecastable rather than a hidden enemy-stat increase.
- The artifact should provide a strong output identity without replacing survival, support, team composition, or ordinary equipment progression.
- Failure must be diagnosable; the pursuit should create preparation choices rather than a single raw-power gate.
- Declining the artifact must not silently remove all early excitement. The safe branch needs an intentional experience, even if the artifact branch is the expected temptation.

## Current Priority

Hold implementation. First use controlled Agent playthroughs of the current two chapters to verify and repair the emotion model. Revisit this event only after the model can explain the current experience reliably.
