# Field Effect Brainstorm 2026-07-07 15:49

Scope: design-only heartbeat round. No source code changes. Three subagents each proposed three field effects using only formal base roles and existing official mechanisms.

This round intentionally avoided all implemented fields and all prior brainstorm families, including combat stat fields, target objectives, boss channels, resistance reversal, loot diagnosis, and reward branching.

The prompt focused on remaining gaps:

- enemy targeting / behavior rules;
- continuous dungeon route planning;
- overuse / waste / efficiency diagnosis.

## Consolidated Pass Candidates

### 1. Glare of Threat

Status: pass as the strongest enemy-behavior candidate.

Core idea:

Enemies periodically look back at the player unit that recently dealt the most damage. A few enemies briefly switch target to that unit. This teaches that output creates threat, and that high-output units need either protection, timing discipline, or distributed damage.

Suggested scope:

- Enemy targeting rule.

Suggested levels:

- L1: every 12 seconds, check the highest-damage player unit over the last 5 seconds. Up to 2 enemies target it for 2 seconds.
- L2: every 10 seconds, target for 2.8 seconds. Up to 3 enemies switch.
- L3: every 8 seconds, target for 3.5 seconds. Up to 4 enemies switch; the target takes +10% damage from the next enemy basic attack.

Favorable teams:

- Multi-core damage teams that spread threat across ranger, mage, warlock, assassin, or berserker.
- Protected single-core teams with knight / priest / bard support.
- Burst-window teams that can time damage, draw threat, then survive the short glare.

Counter / risk:

- Glass-cannon single-core teams may be punished too hard if the target switch ignores taunt, guard, distance, or current frontline rules.
- The rule must be short and visible, not a permanent "kill the carry" behavior.

Player understanding signal:

- After a unit spikes damage, enemy units show a "glare" indicator and briefly turn toward it.
- If another unit spikes next cycle, the glare changes target.
- The player can see that spreading damage or protecting the active carry changes survival.

Difference from existing effects:

- Not `Hunter Fog`: it does not naturally hunt backlines or bypass formation.
- Not `Crown Relay`: it does not reward feeding one carry. It makes carry threat readable.
- Not a stat buff; it rewrites enemy targeting briefly.

Validation:

- Track deaths during glare windows.
- Check that protected single-core and distributed-damage teams both have viable answers.
- Reject if it becomes a hard anti-carry field.

### 2. Forked Danger Road

Status: pass as the strongest continuous-dungeon candidate.

Core idea:

After each fight, the player chooses the next route. Routes change the next fight's rule or pressure, not just the reward. Risky routes can reduce accumulated expedition pressure if won; safe routes preserve pressure but reduce immediate danger.

Suggested scope:

- Multi-fight dungeon route rule.

Suggested levels:

- L1: two route choices. Safe route: next fight is ordinary, but expedition pressure does not clear. Risk route: next fight starts with one extra light enemy; winning clears 1 pressure stack.
- L2: two route choices with pressure tags such as "backline threat", "DOT pressure", "shield shell". Risk route clears 2 pressure stacks but enemy first skill starts 1.5 seconds earlier.
- L3: three choices. Rush: skip a small fight but next fight is elite. Detour: fewer enemies but preserves all pressure. Risk: extra mechanism pressure, win clears 2 pressure stacks and lowers next pressure cap by 1.

Favorable teams:

- Flexible roster players who can adjust team composition after seeing route tags.
- Burst teams that can take risk routes and clear extra enemies quickly.
- Stable teams that use safe or detour routes to manage attrition.

Counter / risk:

- If route tags are vague, the system feels like guessing.
- If risk route rewards are too high, it becomes a mandatory optimal path.
- Needs visible pressure stacks and next-fight preview.

Player understanding signal:

- The player chooses a route and sees the next fight reflect that choice.
- Winning a risk route visibly removes pressure.
- Choosing poorly creates a recognizable problem, such as backline pressure or long-fight attrition.

Difference from existing effects:

- Not `Forked Tribute Chest`: this branches next-fight risk, not reward options.
- Not `Battle Scar Index`: it does not diagnose loot; it creates route-planning decisions.
- Not a single-fight field buff.

Validation:

- Track whether the route choice changes subsequent fight risk and player behavior.
- Reject if safe route is always best or risk route is always best.

### 3. Idle-Cast Calibration

Status: pass as the strongest waste-diagnosis candidate.

Core idea:

The battle detects low-value skill casts such as overhealing, shielding an unpressured target, bursting a target already below kill line, or applying control to an enemy about to die. A small cooldown refund softens the waste while the combat log names the waste category.

Suggested scope:

- Player team diagnostic field.
- Can be mirrored for enemy analysis in tools, but player-facing use should focus on build diagnosis.

Suggested levels:

- L1: low-value cast refunds 8% current cooldown, max once per unit per 10 seconds.
- L2: refunds 13%, max once per unit per 8 seconds.
- L3: refunds 18%, max once per unit per 6 seconds.

Waste categories:

- overheal;
- low-pressure shield;
- burst overkill;
- control on near-dead target;
- DOT / direct-damage kill collision.

Favorable teams:

- Priest / bard / knight support loops with overheal or shield timing issues.
- Mage / ranger / assassin burst teams with overkill collisions.
- Warlock / alchemist DOT teams where direct hits collide with inevitable DOT kills.
- Skill-haste teams where frequent casts can become busy but inefficient.

Counter / risk:

- The refund must be small so players do not intentionally spam bad casts for cooldown value.
- Detection must be conservative; when evidence is unclear, report rather than refund.
- This is more a diagnostic training field than a normal power field.

Player understanding signal:

- A skill cast displays "idle: overheal" or "idle: overkill" when it is low value.
- The cooldown icon bumps slightly backward.
- The post-fight report lists which unit wasted the most skill value.

Difference from existing effects:

- Not `Arcane Tide`: it does not make skill windows stronger.
- Not `Tempo Drum`: it does not reward frequency.
- Not `Battle Scar Index`: it diagnoses skill-use waste during the battle rather than translating scars to loot.

Validation:

- Track waste categories and whether player changes equipment/team to reduce them.
- Reject if high-performing teams gain too much power from refunds.

### 4. Rescue Scent

Status: pass/record as an enemy-behavior subtype.

Core idea:

Enemies briefly turn toward the unit that recently received the most healing or shielding. Healing becomes an intentional target-shaping tool rather than invisible safety.

Suggested levels:

- L1: every 11 seconds, detect highest healing + shield recipient in last 4 seconds. 2 enemies switch for 2 seconds.
- L2: every 9 seconds, 3 enemies switch for 2.6 seconds.
- L3: every 7 seconds, 3 enemies switch for 3.2 seconds; if target is shielded, incoming damage +8%.

Reason to record:

- Good tactical lesson, but it may overlap with shield/healing support systems.
- Best used later as a variant of enemy behavior rules.

### 5. Expedition Pressure

Status: pass/record as an attrition component.

Core idea:

Sloppy or long fights create pressure stacks. Pressure carries into the next fight as unrecovered wounds.

Suggested levels:

- L1: fight over 26 seconds or 90% total team max HP lost adds 1 pressure stack, max 2. Each stack leaves one random ally with 8% unrecovered wound next fight.
- L2: thresholds 23 seconds / 75%, max 3, 10% wound.
- L3: thresholds 20 seconds / 60%, max 4, 12% wound; any death adds +1 stack.

Reason to record:

- It is a useful companion to `Forked Danger Road`.
- Alone, it may feel punitive unless paired with routes that clear pressure.

### 6. Overlight Ledger / Finisher Debt

Status: merge into `Idle-Cast Calibration` as component diagnostics.

Core idea:

- Overhealing is recorded and partially redirected as emergency healing.
- Overkill damage is recorded and partially applied to the next hit.

Reason to merge:

- Both are specific forms of waste diagnosis.
- They are valuable components, but not broad enough as standalone headline field families.

## Duplicate / Rejected Items

- `Commanded Repoint` overlaps with normal targeting and can become a generic anti-single-tank rule. Record only as a possible enemy behavior variant.
- `Afterbattle Echo` is interesting but overlaps with tactical-window memory and may encourage stalling to control the final seconds. Keep as future route modifier, not current pass.
- `Overlight Ledger` and `Finisher Debt` are merged into `Idle-Cast Calibration`.

## Next-Round Forbidden Directions

Do not ask for more variants of:

- Recent-highest-damage threat targeting unless refining `Glare of Threat`.
- Healing/shield recipient enemy targeting unless refining `Rescue Scent`.
- Route branch / risk route / pressure clear choices unless refining `Forked Danger Road`.
- Multi-fight attrition pressure unless refining `Expedition Pressure`.
- Low-value skill-cast detection unless refining `Idle-Cast Calibration`.
- Overheal or overkill ledgers unless treated as components of `Idle-Cast Calibration`.

Carry over all earlier forbidden directions:

- Frontline durability, caster haste, low-health brawl, backline hunting, DOT amplification, shield sustain, attack-speed fields, generic control payoff.
- Carry routing, multi-target pressure, status cleanse, mark economy, tactical banner/window acceleration.
- Lane formation, lone-front formation, death revenge, first blood, last survivor, ultimate delay, cooldown tax, shield-break objective.
- Guarded captain, fragile ritual core, timed kill target, survive timer, no-death contract, emergency low-health fuse, tile explosions, neutral-object protection.
- Boss interrupt channel, timed boss second phase, resistance reversal, damage-type contribution ledger, loot diagnosis, reward branch between sharpen/repair.

## Empty / Underexplored Directions

Worth asking for next:

- Enemy morale / retreat / regroup behavior that changes fight ending, if not overlapping death-order fields.
- Environmental resource fields where players choose when to activate a one-use field object.
- Multi-team or ally-NPC escort fields only after neutral-object support exists.
- Long-term dungeon route modifiers that affect two or three future fights without becoming pure attrition.

## Recommended Priority

1. `Idle-Cast Calibration`: strongest build-efficiency diagnostic; useful for player learning and agent debugging.
2. `Glare of Threat`: strongest enemy targeting behavior; teaches output threat management.
3. `Forked Danger Road`: strongest multi-fight route rule; valuable once dungeon loop exists.
4. `Expedition Pressure`: good component for route systems.
5. `Rescue Scent`: good later variant of behavior-based targeting.
