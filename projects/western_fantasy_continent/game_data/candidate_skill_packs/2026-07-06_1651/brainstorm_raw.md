# Brainstorm Raw Notes

This file records the 32 raw ideas returned by the four subagents. The full text is compressed here into stable design records rather than copied as a chat transcript.

## Low-health Reload / Returning Projectile

1. **断脉弩客**: low-health crossbow carry; three-shot reload; arrows pierce and return; stacks crit, low-HP damage, reload speed, return damage; weak to dive and chip damage.
2. **空膛猎枪**: equipment; empty chamber and last-shot gameplay; shotgun pellets return; stacks magazine size, last-shot damage, reload, projectile count; weak during full-magazine phase.
3. **回钟弹术**: skill; slow projectile marks on outgoing path and detonates on return; stacks mark damage, cooldown, low-HP return speed; weak when positioning is poor.
4. **裂舌火铳手**: low-health firearm carry; self-damaging reload ignites returning fragments; stacks reload payoff, burn, low-HP attack speed; weak without sustain.
5. **倒钩银线**: equipment; arrows drag hidden wire and deal stronger return damage; stacks return frequency, bleed, attack range; weak against close fast enemies.
6. **空伤弹匣**: relic; lost health becomes ammunition; low HP speeds reload and empowers returning projectiles; weak to burst death.
7. **逆羽哨箭**: skill; outgoing arrow is low damage, returning whistle line is high damage; stacks movement, return crit, cooldown; weak if standing still.
8. **黑膛赔命炮**: equipment; slow heavy cannon, return core crits at low HP; stacks heavy damage, reload, low-HP crit, shield-to-damage; weak to fast swarms.

## Shield-to-Damage / Protected Long Cast

1. **辉壁炮手**: shield-fed backline cannon; shield absorption charges a straight-line blast; stacks shield amount, magic power, haste; weak to dispel and interruption.
2. **誓盾咏雷者**: protected thunder chanter; each shield/protection event shortens chant and adds lightning; stacks haste, shield frequency, magic power; weak when protection drops.
3. **裂盾回响杖**: equipment; shield break/expiration releases AOE magic shock; stacks shield quantity and refresh; weak when enemies ignore or dispel shields.
4. **堡垒引信骑士**: front protector/sub-DPS; intercepted damage becomes shield cannon charge; stacks armor, shield power, life, cooldown; weak to bypass damage.
5. **玻璃圣匣术士**: fragile burst caster; records damage while shielded and releases a stored explosion; stacks magic power, shield duration, haste; weak if shield breaks early.
6. **反冲星盘师**: shielded counter turret; shield absorption fires star missiles at attackers; stacks shield refresh, magic power, haste; weak to dispel or enemies pausing fire.
7. **后庭焰冠使**: backline fire carry; nearby frontline shields grant fire crown stacks; stacks magic power, frontline shield, burn damage; weak when formation breaks.
8. **银幕守誓套**: team set; shields on backline accelerate charged skills and add impact; stacks shield power, haste, magic power; weak for instant-cast teams.

## Frost / Mark / Bounce / Backline Hunt

1. **霜弦追猎者**: ranged carry; crit marks targets and bouncing basics splash frost shards; stacks attack speed, crit, bounce count, mark damage; weak versus single armored front.
2. **裂冰双刃**: melee agile carry; repeated hits stack ice cracks and bounce shatter to marked backline; stacks attack speed, crit, frost, mark damage; weak to AOE and counterburst.
3. **寒星弹匣师**: ranged basic core; ice bullets bounce and leave star cracks; crit detonates cracks; stacks attack speed, bounce count, crit; weak when displaced.
4. **碎霜处刑官**: slow heavy shatter output; marked/frosted targets burst ice waves backward; stacks crit damage, frost, mark, armor break; weak without mark setup.
5. **霜鸦猎印者**: summon-synergy shooter; frost bird marks backline and attacks on crit; stacks attack speed, crit, summon damage, mark; weak to AOE clearing summons.
6. **冰棘连弩客**: attack-speed shooter; basics bounce between marked targets and trigger shatter after repeated hits; stacks attack speed, bounce, mark duration; weak before gear.
7. **白霜影枪**: assassin shooter; charges aim on basics then throws to backline mark; stacks crit, mark damage, range; weak versus front-heavy teams.
8. **碎月寒刃轮**: equipment; spinning frost blade bounces, marked targets attract it; stacks bounce, crit, attack speed, trigger rate; weak with too few enemies.

## DOT / Delay / Long Cast

1. **黑钟疫使**: DOT carry; repeated ticks grow and pierce effect resistance, then long-cast bell detonates remaining durations; stacks DOT, duration, penetration, haste; weak to cleanse and burst.
2. **余烬药剂师**: epidemic burn alchemist; deaths transfer remaining DOT and boiling marks detonate after ticks; stacks DOT, duration, haste; weak in spread-out fights.
3. **裂痕咒炮手**: protected long-cast DOT cannon; ticks stack pressure, long cast detonates pressure after delay; stacks haste, protection, DOT, burst; weak to interruption.
4. **瘟火裁缝**: DOT linker; curse thread copies and shares DOT between enemies; stacks duration, DOT, penetration; weak in single-target fights.
5. **焦骨审判官**: slow single-target DOT execution; target survives under judgment and then receives delayed punishment; stacks duration, DOT, penetration; weak against disposable targets.
6. **慢燃圣钉**: equipment; first DOT on elite attaches a nail that ramps tick damage and ends in delayed burst; weak versus short-lived targets.
7. **隔热咒幕**: equipment; long-cast shield converts completed casts into extra DOT marks; stacks haste, cast protection, DOT; weak if shield breaks.
8. **疫火回声戒**: equipment; DOT ticks leave delayed echo bursts with internal cooldown; stacks DOT frequency, haste, penetration; weak to cleanse and immunity.
