# Screening

Applied local project standards:

- skill-kit-design: readable output posture, real build appetite, no duplicate skill roles inside the same kit.
- special-relic-design: width and uplift discipline for equipment/relic ideas.
- design-width-evaluator: current users, plausible future users, and "everyone has this" saturation check.
- phenomenon-math-modeling / comparative-analysis: ask what mathematical pressure the design adds, then compare with previous similar phenomena.

## Counts

- Raw ideas: 32.
- Passed to blind review: 10.
- Recorded for future inspiration: 10.
- Rejected or deferred: 12.

## Passed

1. **断脉弩客**: strongest low-health return-projectile carry; clear reinforcement path.
2. **回钟弹术**: excellent standalone skill/component for returning projectile ecology.
3. **辉壁炮手**: shield as cannon ammunition is instantly readable.
4. **堡垒引信骑士**: protector/sub-DPS that can enable shield-cannon teams.
5. **霜弦追猎者**: clean frost/mark/bounce ranged carry.
6. **寒星弹匣师**: attack-speed bounce fantasy with very clear item appetite.
7. **白霜影枪**: backline mark hunter with good assassin-ranger bridge.
8. **黑钟疫使**: best DOT long-fight carry.
9. **余烬药剂师**: death-transfer DOT spread is readable and useful.
10. **裂痕咒炮手**: protected long-cast DOT cannon links DOT and shield-protection systems.

## Recorded

- **空伤弹匣**: good relic for future low-health reload ecology, but too generic if released before reload users exist.
- **黑膛赔命炮**: strong item fantasy, but overlaps low-health cannon and shield-cannon branches.
- **玻璃圣匣术士**: great risk/reward, but may be too close to 辉壁炮手 in this batch.
- **反冲星盘师**: good counter-turret seed; needs shield-trigger limits to avoid noisy combat.
- **碎月寒刃轮**: useful universal bounce item, but may be too broadly attractive.
- **冰棘连弩客**: clean, but currently redundant with 寒星弹匣师.
- **瘟火裁缝**: thread-link DOT is strong, but needs link visuals before implementation.
- **焦骨审判官**: good single-target slow kill, but short fights may feel bad.
- **慢燃圣钉**: good elite/boss DOT item; save for challenge-reward item design.
- **隔热咒幕**: useful long-cast equipment, but better as support equipment after long-cast users exist.

## Rejected / Deferred

- **空膛猎枪**: fun but weapon-cycle heavy; likely too system-specific for blind character review.
- **裂舌火铳手**: vivid, but self-damage/burn/reload/return has too many first-version hooks.
- **倒钩银线**: attractive visual, but light pull may stress positioning rules.
- **逆羽哨箭**: depends on manual movement/position correction, less suited for current auto-battle.
- **誓盾咏雷者**: protected long-cast is good but overlaps 裂痕咒炮手 and 辉壁炮手.
- **裂盾回响杖**: simple, but looks more like an affix than a blind-review headliner.
- **后庭焰冠使**: strong but formation dependency is currently hard to teach.
- **银幕守誓套**: better as a future set bonus.
- **裂冰双刃**: overlaps assassin mobility and shatter; wait until melee frost branch is stable.
- **碎霜处刑官**: slower heavy frost branch is good but not as clear as ranged bounce.
- **霜鸦猎印者**: summon readability remains a risk.
- **疫火回声戒**: probability echo may be hard to feel in combat without strong VFX.
