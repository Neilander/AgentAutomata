# Field Effect Inspiration Pool

This file records strong field-effect ideas before they are fully tuned. Keep it next to `SKILL.md` so future field-effect work can reuse the taste and avoid losing good prompts.

## Accepted Strong Directions

### Death Inheritance

- Core signal: when a unit dies, a visible soul/light flies to the nearest living ally.
- Rule draft: a dead unit grants a percentage of its power, armor, and max HP to the nearest living ally.
- Why it works: the player immediately asks who should die and who should stand near the death point.
- Likely beneficiaries: martyr front line, summons, front-line sacrifice, protect-one-carry teams.
- Risk: if the percentage is too high, the best answer may become deliberate suicide stacking.
- Validation: compare same-field swaps that add a sacrificial frontliner or a durable carry target.

### Shield Detonation

- Core signal: a unit with a very large shield flashes, detonates, loses shield, and damages nearby enemies.
- Rule draft: when shield exceeds a threshold based on max HP, clear shield and deal damage based on consumed shield.
- Why it works: it turns defensive shield stacking into an active damage plan.
- Likely beneficiaries: knight, priest, shield equipment, defensive front-line teams.
- Risk: if detonation is too efficient, all shield teams become burst teams.
- Validation: compare shield-heavy teams with and without a shield support swap under the same field.

### Wildfire Rings

- Core signal: backline units carry moving fire circles. The circles burn all units inside, including allies.
- Rule draft: each backline unit has a fire circle that expands every few seconds and applies burn to all units in range.
- Why it works: it creates a visible spatial rule and makes the player think about backline spacing, sustain, and dragging enemies through fire.
- Likely beneficiaries: fire/status teams, control teams, healing/sustain teams, teams that can keep enemies in the zone.
- Risk: if the rings grow too fast, the whole battlefield becomes noise and the lesson disappears.
- Validation: compare teams that add control/sustain/status against similar backline teams without the answer.

## Good But Not First Batch

### Backline Echo

- Draft: duplicate backline units at battle start, turning 4v4 into 6v6-like chaos.
- Strength: very high spectacle and easy to understand.
- Risk: it massively amplifies already-strong backline teams and may be better as a late-game special map.

### Undying Altar

- Draft: every 8-12 seconds, a shrine opens and units cannot die for a short duration.
- Strength: clear phase-based combat; burst teams must time around the altar, low-HP teams can exploit it.
- Risk: too much uptime makes combat confusing and drags fights out.

### Healing Militia

- Draft: every X healing done summons a basic warrior for a limited duration, with a summon cap.
- Strength: creates a healing-to-board-presence build.
- Risk: uncontrolled summons slow the fight and make priest mandatory.

### Perpetual Valley

- Draft: after 10 seconds, units freeze and can only basic attack their own ice. After dealing enough basic damage, they break out.
- Strength: creates a dramatic phase shift that favors attack speed/basic attack teams.
- Risk: needs strong visual clarity and may pause too much of the normal combat.
